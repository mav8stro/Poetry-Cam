POETIC ETHICS & GOVERNANCE

Ethics, Folk Lore & Privacy

CANON OF ARTISTIC RESPONSIBILITY

Poetry as Sacred Witness, Not Surveillance

VerseCam adheres to the doctrine of ethical AI: human beings, sacred environments, and personal moments are never commodities for algorithmic extraction.

01

Folk Lore, Oral Balladry & Cultural Reverence

VerseCam integrates centuries-old oral folk lore and balladry structures (Appalachian, Celtic, and pastoral refrains). Our neural models prioritize communal storytelling, honoring elder dignities, weathered tools, rustic hearths, and the quiet resilience of working lives without caricature or exoticization.

02

Ethical Grace & Non-Degradation

The CNN-RNN sequence generator is constrained by strict anti-derogatory lyrical filters. Visual tokens depicting physical differences, imperfections, or vulnerability are embraced with profound compassion and poetic elevation rather than clinical objectification.

03

Nudity & Modesty Gating (Safety Guardian)

Our client-side modesty guardian flags sensitive physical exposure before verses are generated, giving the user immediate artistic controls: apply an impressionist watercolor blur, re-frame the camera, or explicitly confirm creative consent.

04

Algorithmic Hallucination Boundaries

Using Non-Maximum Suppression (NMS) and Spatial Scene Graphs, poetic metaphors are strictly anchored to genuine scene elements, preventing disjointed or misleading hallucinations.

ZERO CLOUD FOOTPRINT

100% In-Browser Edge Processing

Your camera feed is your sovereign sanctuary. VerseCam operates entirely in local device memory with zero cloud ingestion.

01

No Image Data Ingestion or Transmission

No raw pixels, video frames, or captured photos are ever transmitted to any remote server or external cloud storage. Detection models (TensorFlow.js COCO-SSD) run strictly within your browser WebGL / WebAssembly sandbox.

02

Local Storage Sandbox

Saved verses, lyric drafts, and polaroid scrapbooks remain strictly inside your browser’s indexed localStorage database on your device. You can purge all data at any moment with a single click.

03

Zero Tracking, Ads, or Third-Party Pixels

VerseCam contains no tracking beacons, telemetry trackers, or advertising SDKs. What you capture remains solely between you and the poem.
